CHAPTER 3 · THE SHEEP
The Sheep
ANTOINE DE SAINT-EXUPÉRY · 双语对照 · 约 4 分钟

It was from the man thrown amidst the sands a thousand miles from any inhabited region that the little prince made the acquaintance of a sheep.

小王子就是在这个千里之外、无人烟的沙漠里，遇见了那只小羊。

"Please—draw me a sheep," he said, with a gesture of utmost gravity.

"请你——给我画一只羊，"他说，神情无比认真。

I looked around. There was nothing but sand, and a sky full of stars at dusk.

我环顾四周，只有沙子和黄昏时满是星星的天空。

THE CONVERSATION · B2
Why your brain needs boredom to do its best thinking
BY CLARA HUGHES · 11月5日 · 8 MIN · 已读 2/8 MIN

We treat boredom like a bug in the system — something to be swiped away the moment it appears.

我们把无聊当作系统漏洞——一出现就想立刻划掉它。

But neuroscience suggests the idle brain is anything but idle. In the quiet moments, a network called the default mode switches on and begins its slow, creative work.

但神经科学告诉我们，闲置的大脑绝非真的闲置。在安静的片刻，一个叫"默认模式网络"的系统悄然开启，开始它缓慢而富有创造性的工作。

The next time you feel bored, try doing nothing at all. Your best ideas may be waiting in the silence.

下次觉得无聊时，试着什么都不做。你最好的想法，也许就藏在沉默里。

WORD CARDS · 本文生词 3/12
boredom/ˈbɔːrdəm/
n. 无聊，乏味 — treat boredom like a bug（把无聊当作漏洞）
